Xiaomi Bluetooth Remote 2 / 2 Pro

Model: RC001 / RC003 · Direct Bluetooth
Supported
  • 2 vs. 2 Pro: Remote 2 (RC001) and 2 Pro (RC003) share the same voice, button, and Bluetooth functionality. The main difference is power—Remote 2 uses replaceable batteries, while 2 Pro has a built-in 300mAh battery charged via bottom USB-C.
  • Build: Slim brushed-metal body (2 Pro 138 × 35 × 8.9 mm, ~61 g), circular D-pad, and crisp tactile keys.
  • Voice trigger: Hold the voice button to speak and release to finish (~60s single-hold cap). Crisp walkie-talkie feel with no need to remember a second press to stop.
  • Button mapping: Remappable single, double, and long presses. Defaults: Home switches apps, TV plays/pauses media, Menu opens context menu, Power sends Backspace, Back sends Escape.
  • Buying note: 2 Pro is listed around ¥99 CNY (~$14 USD; ~¥60+ CNY on sales) in mainland China. If buying Remote 2, verify the model label reads RC001 rather than an IR-only variant.

T1 Air-Mouse & Keyboard Remote

Model: T1-Remote · Direct Bluetooth (not 2.4G dongle)
Sample verified
  • 3-in-1 hardware: 164 × 50 × 11 mm, ~62 g (400mAh, right-side USB-C). Front features 7 remappable buttons (Back, Home, Menu, Vol+, Vol−, Mute, Power); rear features a ~45-key QWERTY mini keyboard plus a 6-axis gyro air mouse passing through natively to macOS.
  • Hands-free toggle: On the tested Bluetooth sample, press once to start talking, then press again to stop (tested nearly 2 minutes continuously; keepalive every 10s up to ~5 min protective cutoff). Works seamlessly with both Hold-mode and Toggle-mode input tools.
  • Buying note: Commonly ~¥50 CNY (~$7 USD) on Taobao. Hold Home + Back to pair over direct Bluetooth (2.4 GHz USB receivers do not carry ATVV audio). Lookalike OEM batches vary—buy from a seller offering returns.

Google Chromecast Voice Remote

Model: G9N9N · 2× AAA batteries
Partial (Voice + Combo)
  • Voice behavior: Hold the Google Assistant button for the entire sentence; audio stops immediately upon release (always use Hold mode).
  • Button restriction: Tested original sample has HID report-length quirks preventing single-button remapping. When mapping is active, short-pressing OK + any direction sends Enter.
  • Compatible clones: Users reported ~¥15 CNY (~$2 USD) compatible units on Pinduoduo (Pinduoduo Listing ↗) with working voice and basic keys, though AAA battery drain is faster and firmware behavior varies by batch.

CURRENTLY USABLE DEVICES · VISUAL COMPARISON

Three-remote comparison

Xiaomi Bluetooth Remote 2 Pro, Google Chromecast G9N9N, and T1 Air-Mouse & Keyboard Voice Remote all provide voice input, with different physical voice-button behavior and ordinary-button support.

SIDE-BY-SIDE COMPARISON

Xiaomi Remote 2 / 2 Pro vs. T1 Air-Mouse Remote: Which should you choose?

Both deliver clear 16 kHz IMA-ADPCM speech via ATVV v1.0, but differ dramatically in size, extra hardware (air mouse + mini keyboard), and push-to-talk vs. toggle voice workflow.

Front view: Xiaomi 2 Pro (left) has a slim metal finish; T1 (right) is wider and moves volume + USB-C to its right edge.

Port placement: Xiaomi places USB-C on the bottom end, whereas T1 places USB-C and volume keys along the right edge.

Footprint: Xiaomi 2 Pro measures 138 × 35 × 8.9 mm; T1 measures 164 × 50 × 11 mm. Xiaomi is noticeably shorter and narrower.

Thickness: Xiaomi 2 Pro is 8.9 mm thick, compared with 11 mm for T1. Their port placement also differs.

T1 Dual-Sided Layout & Controls

The front face provides 7 remappable control keys (Back, Home, Menu, Vol+, Vol−, Mute, Power) for triggering custom shortcuts while keeping D-pad and OK native. Flip the remote over to type on a ~45-key QWERTY mini keyboard or steer the Mac pointer with its built-in 6-axis gyro air mouse.

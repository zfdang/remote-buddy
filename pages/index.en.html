CLOSE PICKUP · FLEXIBLE PACE

Smart Toggle for long prompts, hold for quick replies

Dictate naturally from your chair without leaning into your laptop mic. G9N9N defaults to Smart Toggle: tap once for hands-free listening (exceeding 60s via keepalive) and tap to stop, or hold past 0.7s to stop on release.

PER-APP PROFILES

Scroll Safari & Chrome, send messages in one click

Map single, double, and long presses or common phrases across 14 physical keys, and override per app: Safari and Chrome presets turn the D-pad into smooth scroll & Back/Forward, while chats use Mute/OK to send with Return or Command+Return.

SMART FOCUS

Finds your chat box—never steals an active cursor

Optional integrations for WeChat, Antigravity, ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, and WorkBuddy locate the conversation input if nothing is focused, while keeping any field you already selected; pairs seamlessly with your preferred voice tool.

BUDGET-FRIENDLY · LOCAL PRIVACY

Budget hardware, multi-remote labels, zero storage

Starts with pocket-friendly remotes like G9N9N, with custom device names and per-remote mic gain (0–24 dB). Audio is decoded solely in memory with zero saved history or telemetry, GPL-3.0-only, on macOS and Windows.

PRIVACY, SECURITY & INSTALLATION

Open source, local-only audio bridging—zero saved recordings or text history.

Remote Buddy Itself

  • No audio or transcript storage: Bluetooth audio is decoded solely in local memory and routed to the virtual microphone. Remote Buddy runs no speech model and stores no audio recordings or recognized text.
  • Zero telemetry or background uploads: No usage analytics, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no automatic log uploads (diagnostic logs remain strictly local unless you inspect or copy them).
  • 100% open source: Both the macOS app and the bundled Remote Buddy Mic virtual audio driver are published under GPL-3.0-only so anyone can inspect or build from source.
  • Strict API boundaries: Uses only public macOS integration and user-visible Accessibility APIs, never touching other apps’ private data.

Your Chosen Input Method / Voice Tool (Separate Scope)

Once audio leaves Remote Buddy Mic, speech recognition and text insertion are handled independently by the input tool you select. Whether recognition stays offline or uses cloud servers depends strictly on that tool’s own settings and privacy policy—Remote Buddy neither participates in nor reads its recognition output. For a completely offline end-to-end setup on Apple Silicon, pair it with Voice Buddy.

Installation & signing notes: Installing or updating the bundled Remote Buddy Mic driver requires administrator authorization and briefly restarts macOS audio. The PKG installer is currently not notarized with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS Gatekeeper may ask you to allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security (see Build & Signing Notes ↗).
